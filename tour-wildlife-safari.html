📍 On-The-Way Sightseeing Places

  • Theppakadu Elephant Camp (Mudumalai): Famous elephant feeding camp featured in Oscar-winning documentary 'The Elephant Whisperers'.
  • Moyar River & Masinagudi Forest Corridor: Prime wildlife corridor frequently inhabited by wild elephants, gaurs, chitals, and peacocks.
  • Thamarassery Churam (9 Hairpin Bends): Dramatic mountain pass connecting Wayanad with Kozhikode/Calicut.
  • Edakkal Caves & Banasura Sagar Dam: Prehistoric petroglyph cave drawings and Asia's largest earth dam.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: What wildlife sanctuaries are included in this tour package from Coimbatore?

The tour covers Mudumalai Tiger Reserve, Bandipur National Park, and Anamalai Wildlife Sanctuary (Topslip).

Q2: What are the forest checkpost timings for Mudumalai & Bandipur?

Forest gates operate strictly between 6:00 AM and 6:00 PM. Night driving is prohibited to protect wildlife.
