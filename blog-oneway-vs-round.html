Oneway Cabs vs Round Trip: Save Up to 40% On Intercity Travel

📅 Published: Feb 18, 2026 ⏱️ Read Time: 4 min ✍️ By Get Cabs Tariff Analysts

When traveling outstation from Coimbatore to nearby cities like Tiruppur, Erode, Salem, Palakkad, or Chennai, traditional taxi operators force customers to pay double-sided round-trip mileage meters (2x distance billing) even if you only need a drop-off to your destination.

How Oneway Drop Taxi Billing Works

With Get Cabs Coimbatore fixed one-way drop taxis, you pay ONLY for the distance traveled from your pickup location to your destination point. We utilize return passenger network routing to absorb the return empty leg, saving you 30% to 40% on total fare!
