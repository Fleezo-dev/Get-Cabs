📜 Billing Rules & Exclusions Explained

  • Toll Gate Fees: Toll gate charges are extra at actuals (Fastag) and payable by passenger.
  • Parking & State Permits: Airport parking, commercial parking lots, and interstate permits (e.g. Kerala / Karnataka permit) are at actuals.
  • Driver Batta: Daily driver batta is ₹300 for plains routes and ₹500 for hill station routes (Ooty, Munnar, Kodaikanal, Valparai).
  • Outskirts Drop Surcharge: Outer Coimbatore areas (Karumathampatti, Karanampettai, Paapampatti, Kovilpalayam, Karamadai, Ettimadai, Kinathukadavu) carry a nominal adjustment (+₹100 to ₹150).
