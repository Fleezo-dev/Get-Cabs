Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

Get Cabs Coimbatore operates the website https://www.getcabs.in and commercial taxi booking services in Tamil Nadu, India. We are committed to safeguarding your personal privacy and data.

1. Information We Collect

When you book a cab or request a fare estimate, we collect minimal information necessary for dispatch:

  • Name and contact telephone / WhatsApp number
  • Pickup location, destination drop point, date and departure time
  • Vehicle preferences (Hatchback, Sedan, SUV, Tempo Traveller)

2. How Your Data Is Used

Your details are used solely to dispatch your ride, coordinate with your assigned chauffeur, and send driver and vehicle tracking updates via SMS/WhatsApp.

3. Data Protection Guarantee

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal phone numbers or trip itineraries with third-party marketing entities.
