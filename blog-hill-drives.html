Top 5 Scenic Hill Station Road Trips from Coimbatore (2026 Guide)

📅 Published: Feb 24, 2026 ⏱️ Read Time: 7 min ✍️ By Get Cabs Mountain Chauffeurs

Coimbatore is uniquely positioned at the foothills of the Western Ghats, making it the supreme launchpad for scenic mountain drives. Here are the top 5 hill station road trips along with distance, hairpin bends, and cab recommendations:

1. Valparai (105 KM - 40 Hairpin Bends)

Famous for the dramatic 40 hairpin curves winding past Aliyar Dam, Loam's View Point, and Carver Marsh statue. Expect sightings of the endangered Nilgiri Tahr and lion-tailed macaques.

2. Ooty & Coonoor (87 KM - 36 Hairpin Curves)

The queen of hill stations! Ascend through the Kallar ghat road and experience rapid temperature drops, tea estate panoramas, and Doddabetta Peak.

3. Munnar (160 KM - Western Ghats Tea Corridors)

Travel via Udumalpet and Marayoor sandalwood reserve. Enjoy Cheeyappara waterfalls, Mattupetty dam, and sprawling tea estates.
