Best Hill Station Drives from Coimbatore: Valparai, Kodaikanal & Munnar

📅 Published: Feb 25, 2026 ⏱️ Read Time: 6 min ✍️ By Get Cabs Mountain Fleet Team

Nestled at the foot of the Nilgiris and Anamalai ranges, Coimbatore serves as the gateway to South India's finest hill stations. Whether you want to conquer the 40 hairpin curves of Valparai or relax amidst Munnar's tea plantations, having an experienced mountain driver ensures maximum safety and enjoyment.

1. Valparai (40 Hairpin Curves & Lion-Tailed Macaques)

Located 105 KM from Coimbatore via Pollachi and Aliyar Dam, the drive to Valparai climbs through 40 challenging hairpin bends. Key attractions include Loam's View Point, Monkey Falls, Sholayar Dam, and tea plantations where rare Lion-Tailed Macaques roam freely.

2. Munnar (Waterfalls & Spice Country)

Situated 160 KM away via Udumalpet and Marayoor, Munnar offers cool mountain air, Chinnar wildlife sanctuary crossings, and Cheeyappara waterfalls.

3. Kodaikanal (Princess of Hill Stations)

A 175 KM drive takes you to the serene star-shaped Kodai Lake, Pillar Rocks, and Silver Cascade waterfalls.
