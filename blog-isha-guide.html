Coimbatore to Isha Yoga & Adiyogi: Taxi Guide, Distance & Rates (2026)

📅 Published: Mar 01, 2026 ⏱️ Read Time: 5 min ✍️ By Get Cabs Pilgrimage Desk

The Isha Yoga Center, nestled at the foothills of the Velliangiri Mountains, is one of the most visited spiritual landmarks in South India. Home to the towering 112-foot Adiyogi statue and the consecrated Dhyanalinga, it attracts visitors from across the globe daily.

Route & Distance from Coimbatore

Distance from Coimbatore Gandhipuram or Railway Station is approximately 33 KM (45-50 minutes), while from Coimbatore Airport (CJB) it is approximately 42 KM (1 hour).

Cab Tariff from Coimbatore to Isha Yoga

  • One-Way Drop (Sedan AC): ₹1,100 fixed
  • Round-Trip Sightseeing (With 3-4 Hours Wait): ₹1,800 fixed
  • CJB Airport to Isha Yoga (One Way): ₹1,400 fixed
  • SUV (Ertiga / Innova 6-7 Seater Round Trip): ₹2,500 fixed

Attending Adiyogi Divya Darshanam Light Show?

The 3D Laser Projection show runs at 7:00 PM and 8:00 PM daily. Book your return cab in advance!
