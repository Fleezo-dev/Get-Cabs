Top 10 Places to Visit in Ooty from Coimbatore (2026 Travel Guide)

📅 Published: Jan 15, 2026 ⏱️ Read Time: 6 min ✍️ By Get Cabs Travel Desk

Ooty (Udhagamandalam), the Queen of Hill Stations, is located just 85 KM away from Coimbatore, making it one of the most popular weekend getaways and tourist destinations in South India. Driving from Coimbatore to Ooty takes you through scenic plains, lush tea gardens, and the famous 36 Hairpin Curves ghat road via Mettupalayam and Coonoor.

1. Doddabetta Peak – Highest Point in the Nilgiris

Standing tall at 2,637 meters above sea level, Doddabetta Peak offers breathtaking 360-degree views of the entire Nilgiri mountain range and Bandipur forest valleys. The peak features an observatory with telescopes for tourists.

2. Government Botanical Garden & Rose Garden

Spread across 55 acres, the Botanical Garden houses over a thousand species of exotic flora, a fossilized tree trunk estimated to be 20 million years old, and manicured lawns perfect for family walks.

3. Ooty Lake & Boating

An artificial lake constructed in 1824, Ooty Lake offers paddle boating, motor boating, row boating, and a charming toy train ride around the perimeter.

4. Pykara Lake, Waterfalls & Pine Forests

Located 21 KM from Ooty towards Mysore, Pykara features pristine waterfalls, speed boating in deep green lake waters, and dense towering pine forests popular for film shootings.

5. Sim's Park & Dolphin's Nose (Coonoor)

En route between Mettupalayam and Ooty, stop at Coonoor to explore Sim's Park, a rare botanical garden featuring unusual tree species, and Dolphin's Nose viewpoint overlooking Catherine Falls.

📋 Nilgiris E-Pass Rules for 2026

As per Madras High Court directives, all private vehicles and outstation cabs entering Nilgiris (Ooty / Coonoor / Kotagiri) must generate a free e-pass via pass.tnega.org before passing the Mettupalayam or Katteri checkposts.
