Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find quick answers to common questions about tariffs, booking steps, vehicle types, and outstation trips.

1. How do I book a cab in Coimbatore with Get Cabs?

You can book directly by calling our 24/7 hotline at 9894020156, messaging us on WhatsApp, or using the instant fare calculator on our homepage.

2. What is the difference between one-way drop and round trip?

In one-way drops, you pay only for the single journey distance to your destination with zero return charges. In round trips, the cab stays with you and bills on total round-trip distance (minimum 250 KM/day) plus driver batta.

3. Are toll charges and parking included in the fare?

No, toll taxes (Fastag) and parking fees are paid at actuals based on your specific route.

4. Can I get a cab with a carrier for extra luggage?

Yes, we have Sedans and SUVs equipped with top luggage carriers. Please notify us during booking if you have bulky baggage.
